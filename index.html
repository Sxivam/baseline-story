The realisation

A friend's pre-op bloodwork started this.

Before a minor surgery, his doctor ordered a basic panel. He booked it on a pharmacy app for a few hundred rupees. I didn't even know testing was that cheap — and I care about this more than most.

So I ran it on myself. I'm the fittest in my group: gym daily, clean diet, cardio. I expected a clean sheet. I didn't get one. You can't know what's happening inside you from how you look or how much you lift.

Then I made my whole friend group test.

Ten high performers tested. 10 out of 10 came back with hidden issues — quietly draining their energy, mood and focus.

One friend's vitamin D was barely survivable, while she blamed her consulting job for the exhaustion. In a group built for being driven and health-aware, the deficiency wasn't the exception. It was the default.

The problem

People optimise what they can see and ignore what they can't. Even when something makes them test once, nothing keeps them in the loop — so the issues silently return.

The approach

So the product isn't a test. It's the loop the test should trigger.
